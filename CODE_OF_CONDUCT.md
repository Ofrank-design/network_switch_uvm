# Code of Conduct

This project is for people who care about getting hardware verification right.
Everyone taking part is expected to keep it a place where that work can happen.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the code and the argument, not the person.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take feedback the same way.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing private information without consent.
- Sexual attention or imagery.
- Continued disruption of discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where
someone is acting on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to Frank Oduro at frankoduro1912@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately and in good faith. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close
threads, and block people who do not follow these rules. The reporter's identity
is not shared without their consent.
